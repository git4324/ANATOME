# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People documenting pain before a clinical conversation and clinicians reviewing structured patient-reported information. The primary workflow is identifying a precise body region, recording symptoms, and producing a clear report.

## Product Purpose

ANATOME turns spatial pain input and questionnaire answers into a structured, non-diagnostic record that improves communication without replacing professional assessment.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, precise, calm.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-dashboard styling, decorative emoji, game-like anatomy interactions, alarmist medical language, and motion that competes with the assessment task.

## Design Principles

- Make anatomical selection spatially precise and immediately understandable.
- Use motion only to preserve orientation or explain a state change.
- Keep clinical information legible, restrained, and free of decorative noise.
- Preserve patient-reported meaning and uncertainty throughout the workflow.
- Treat privacy and accessibility as product behavior, not finishing work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard-visible controls. Do not rely on color alone for selection or pain severity. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep the workflow usable when camera animation is disabled.
